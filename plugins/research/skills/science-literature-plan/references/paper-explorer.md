# Paper-explorer assignment

Assign one narrow method, benchmark, dataset, theoretical, implementation-lineage, or negative-result
slice. Cap one explorer at 3-4 primary sources. Provide the research question, active claim,
evaluation boundary, and output path; do not provide the desired conclusion.

Require the explorer to return:

1. Source identities and links inspected.
2. Author claims, measured evidence, and the explorer's interpretation as separate fields.
3. Method, data, metrics, baselines, protocol, and exact implementation-relevant details.
4. Results, limitations, threats to validity, paper/code drift, and reproduction blockers.
5. What the sources do not establish and contradictions with other assigned evidence.
6. Suggested follow-up sources outside its slice, without reading them itself.
7. Tools/commands used, files touched, and unresolved risks.

The coordinator owns deduplication, contradiction resolution, synthesis, and recommendations.
